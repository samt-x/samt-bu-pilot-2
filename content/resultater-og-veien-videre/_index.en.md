The pilot was carried out from May to August 2026, and the results were presented at the demo on 28 August. It delivered a basis for decision, not a solution. The proposal for the way forward is a draft.

## Main results

- **The current situation has been mapped** for vilbli.no and utdanning.no: ownership, editorial work, data flow and use. Most visits to vilbli.no go to content that is also found on utdanning.no. See [Current situation](/en/pilotering/pilot-2/naasituasjon/).
- **33 stakeholders have been identified** and assessed by interest and influence.
- **Three scenarios** have been described, and the pilot recommends investigating a merger of vilbli.no and utdanning.no under HK-dir, with the county authorities retaining control over their own content. See [Choice of solution](/en/pilotering/pilot-2/loesningsvalg/).
- **Architecture principles** have been proposed, with the Education Register (Utdanningsregisteret) as the shared core for data about the education programmes.
- **Legal questions** about procurement law and state aid have been identified, but not clarified.

## Possible benefits

These benefits should be looked at more closely and quantified:

- one place for the user to deal with, instead of two, and seamless services
- better quality of information and better coherence between the information about education
- clearer lines of responsibility for the content
- more efficient use of resources, lower total operating costs and less duplicate work maintaining information on several websites
- simpler data flow, and a data-centric and machine-readable service in the background
- a better basis for AI services and personalised services

The financial consequences have not yet been described.

## The way forward

The benefits must be investigated and quantified further, and the legal questions must be clarified. A decision on vilbli.no lies with FKU. The experience from the pilot shall also provide input to the project's [working model](/en/styring/arbeidsmodell/) for pilots.
