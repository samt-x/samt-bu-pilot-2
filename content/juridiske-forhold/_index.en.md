Legal questions that must be clarified before a merger of vilbli.no and utdanning.no can be decided. They have not been investigated in the pilot.

## Ownership today

- **vilbli.no** is owned by the county authorities. Novari IKS is responsible for procurement and management of the existing agreement.
- **utdanning.no** is owned by HK-dir, which also manages and operates the solution.

## What must be clarified

- **Procurement law:** whether Novari, within its procurement regulations and the framework of procurement law, can enter into an agreement with HK-dir.
- **State aid law:** whether a state agency can take over a digital service that is currently exposed to competition. The pilot refers to [a previous judgment of the EFTA Court](https://eftacourt.int/download/1-12-judgment/?wpdmdl=1103) in a similar case.

HK-dir's legal department has given the pilot more information about what it means for one agency to take over another's service.

vilbli.no is not based on personal data, and there is no data processing agreement between Novari IKS and the supplier.
