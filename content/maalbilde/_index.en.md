## Desired situation

One coherent public website brings together all education and career information, with common terminology, a clear connection between education, competence, occupations, businesses and the labour market, and easy access to neutral career guidance. The service is managed by a single actor with clear lines of responsibility, one editorial team and one technical solution. Users looking for information about upper secondary education find everything in one place, without having to navigate between separate services.

## What a future solution should achieve

The pilot has gathered these objectives for a future solution:

- the user at the centre, with one source of information instead of two
- improved presentation of data
- simpler and better data flow
- lower costs for operation and maintenance
- facilitation for AI agents that will use open data
- adaptation to statutory changes
- personalised content for logged-in users, for example by place of residence, interest tests or school results

The target picture has not been fully investigated. How it can be achieved is described under [Choice of solution](/en/pilotering/pilot-2/loesningsvalg/).
