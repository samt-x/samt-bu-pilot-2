The pilot's proposals for architecture, information model and data sharing for a future solution. The proposals are high-level and have not been investigated in detail.

## Architecture principles

The pilot recommends continuing work on these principles:

- Data shall be machine-readable.
- The Education Register (Utdanningsregisteret) should form the basis for shared data and contain the entire core structure.
- The school offerings from VIGO should go via the Education Register, as a central version of the education programmes.
- The work should follow the [Framework for digital collaboration](https://www.digdir.no/digital-samhandling/rammeverk-digital-samhandling/2148).

## Information model

The data should be prepared so that it can more easily be displayed and used by AI systems that will use open data. The Data Sharing Act (datadelingsloven), on the re-use of public data, shall also be assessed (see [the guide to open data](https://data.norge.no/guide/veileder-apne-data)).

## New addresses

A shared solution will affect users who have bookmarked fixed addresses. The following must be considered:

- what new addresses mean for search engine optimisation, since today's pages rank highly
- redirection from existing to new addresses, and marketing of the change
- existing posters and printouts with addresses, so that new addresses must be made known
