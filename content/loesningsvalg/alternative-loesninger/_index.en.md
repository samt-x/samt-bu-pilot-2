The pilot has described three possible scenarios for vilbli.no. They should be assessed against user needs, legal scope for action, architecture, finances, feasibility and consequences for existing organisations and solutions.

## A: vilbli.no continues as a stand-alone service

vilbli.no is owned by the county authorities through FKU and procured, managed and further developed by Novari IKS as today. This is the zero alternative: a new procurement in the usual way, and two websites side by side in the future as well.

Consequences:

- The solution is procured, managed and further developed as it has been for almost 20 years.
- The agreement runs until 2027 and can be extended by up to three years (1+1+1). A new procurement must therefore be carried out within a few years, probably in 2029 at the latest.
- The service will probably need modernisation at the new procurement, in light of technological developments.
- It will probably have to be considered how data-centric a modernised vilbli.no should be, in light of the work in SAMT-BU.
- A needs analysis will probably be required before the new procurement, in light of AI, enormous amounts of available information and the need for quality-assured and possibly personalised information.
- The county authorities will want efficient and financially sound services.

## B: One shared user channel with several suppliers

One shared user channel with several underlying suppliers of data and content. This is an entirely new solution, different from today's. The pilot does not know what it would involve or whether it is relevant, and has not investigated the scenario. Other actors may take it further.

## C: One combined service

vilbli.no and utdanning.no are merged into one service with shared service ownership, editorial team and technical solution, in cooperation with HK-dir. This is the scenario the pilot recommends, see [Recommended solution](/en/pilotering/pilot-2/loesningsvalg/anbefalt-loesning/).
