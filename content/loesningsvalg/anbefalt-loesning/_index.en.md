The pilot recommends investigating a merger of vilbli.no and utdanning.no into one service, managed by HK-dir and integrated into utdanning.no (scenario C under [Alternative solutions](/en/pilotering/pilot-2/loesningsvalg/alternative-loesninger/)). HK-dir would then take over editorial responsibility from Novari IKS. The recommendation is a draft from the pilot and has not been decided. FKU owns vilbli.no and decides.

A new solution must cover what today's solutions cover, including content in several languages and a poster, printout and PDF of the graphical view of the education pathways.

## The county authorities retain control over their own content

A key question is whether the county authorities retain real control over the content, or whether HK-dir in practice takes over control. HK-dir has an established model for partner access on utdanning.no, where the institutions log in themselves and edit their own content. It is already used by vocational colleges, university colleges and universities, and by all the county authorities for the Upper secondary for adults (Videregående for voksne) offering. The same principle can be extended to the content on vilbli.no.

On the partner pages, a local editor (school or county authority) would be able to:

- edit descriptions of education offerings
- add, edit, remove, publish and unpublish offerings
- add offerings per campus, data that is missing today
- upload images and video
- see version history and roll back in case of errors
- preview the page as the end user sees it, and share the preview with a colleague
- translate content into other written standards and languages, for example Nynorsk, Sami or English

**Login** takes place in a separate partner portal, either with username and password or with Feide or ID-porten. Access is granted per county authority unit: the editors see and edit only their own county's content, and the access structure can mirror today's organisation, so that editorial authority is not centralised.

**Traceability:** all changes are logged with user and time. The county authorities can have a super-editor who can override changes from the schools in their own county. The editors can receive email notifications of changes, and data is to be checked and confirmed at least once a year.

**Guidance:** guidance material, a support channel where the county authorities quickly get help from HK-dir, and regular editorial gatherings.

## Collaboration model

The county authorities own the local content, while HK-dir owns the data structure and the publishing platform. The counties are responsible for the quality, relevance and updating of the content about the county and local offerings. HK-dir is responsible for technical management, structure, standards and an overall editorial framework, and assists with editing in consultation with each county authority. The schools can still update and supply information. This combines local ownership with national consistency.

Today, resources are spent operating several systems with partly overlapping content. A merger could free up full-time equivalents for editorial work and service development. Existing meetings, annual cycles and procedures should be reused where appropriate.

## Better data quality

The county authorities have been concerned that the information on utdanning.no is not always correct or up to date. Much of the challenge lies in the data flow: utdanning.no has received data from vilbli.no as an XML export, with no indicator of whether the counties have finished entering their data. Incomplete datasets have therefore been imported and published. With one shared platform, updates become visible immediately, and status indicators can be built in showing whether a county's data is complete and ready for publication.

## Further process

The solution is deliberately not fully defined. The county authorities' actual needs for entering, updating and presenting data must be mapped thoroughly before the technical choices are made. If it is decided to merge the services, a design process is envisaged in which the county authorities and end users such as pupils, apprentices, career counsellors and teachers take part in shaping the solution, with iterative user testing. HK-dir has good experience of such co-creation with the county authorities from the work on Upper secondary for adults (Videregående for voksne).
